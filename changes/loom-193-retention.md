### Added

- A retention sweep deletes rows the server no longer needs (LOOM-193): sessions 7 days after they expired (by the sliding window or the absolute lifetime), finished dispatches 90 days after they finished, and answered or expired confirmations 30 days after. A queued or running dispatch and a pending confirmation are never deleted, and messages are always kept (a deleted dispatch's messages stay, no longer naming it). `LOOMUX_SESSION_RETENTION`, `LOOMUX_DISPATCH_RETENTION` and `LOOMUX_CONFIRMATION_RETENTION` set each (a duration; `0` keeps the rows). It deletes in batches of 500 rows, each its own transaction, so a first sweep over months of history never holds the database's write lock for long. It runs at startup and then hourly, logs `retention sweep` with what it deleted, and is counted in `loomux_retention_deleted_total{table}`, `loomux_retention_errors_total{table}` and `loomux_retention_last_sweep_timestamp_seconds`.
