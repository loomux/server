### Added

- A client can take over a task while a person is attached to its pane, and hand it back (LOOM-264, the terminal client's S3). `POST /api/v1/tasks/{id}/takeover` `{mode?, client_label?, ttl_seconds?, force?}` gives the caller's session a hold on the task and returns `{task_id, hold, attach}`, the attach-info with it. The task moves to `human_takeover`, which pauses automation into its pane. The lease is 15 to 300 seconds, 45 by default. `POST /api/v1/tasks/{id}/takeover/heartbeat` `{hold_id}` renews it, and `POST /api/v1/tasks/{id}/release` `{hold_id?}` ends it and returns the task to `awaiting_input` with its stale prompt cleared. Release without `hold_id` is Take back: any session may end whatever hold the task has.
- The hold is checked before the task's status. The same session taking again gets its own hold back, renewed. Another session gets `409` with code `task_held` and the hold, unless it sends `force`, which ends the other hold as `taken_over`. A `failed` task with `error_class` `login_required`, `agent_rate_limited` or `agent_api_error` can be held and stays `failed`. Any other ended task is `409` `task_inactive`. `mode: "watch"` is `400` `unsupported_mode` for now, and is never answered with a drive hold. A heartbeat on an ended hold is `410` `hold_ended` with `ended_reason`.
- Not in this change: a hold doesn't expire by itself yet and nothing detaches the holder's tmux clients when it ends (S4), a turn in flight on a taken task runs to its own end before it notices (S5), and holds aren't shown on conversations or the stream yet (S6).
