### Added

- Kubernetes machines can have egress `trusted` (LOOM-226): the internet, the LAN and the tailnet, for the machines an admin designates, such as a command-center hub. It needs three things: the cluster's NetworkPolicy for `loomux.io/egress=trusted` (design §10 item 6 (e), `agents-allow-trusted` in the kind manifests), the plugin's new `trusted_machines` setting naming the target, and `egress: trusted` asked for at create. It is never the default. The host and the plugin log every trusted machine and pod. Taking a name out of `trusted_machines` turns the machine to `error` (the host hears of it at once) and refuses its start and recreate. Check reports `trusted_egress_policy_missing` when no policy lets trusted machines out. Egress `none` and its checks are unchanged.
